Lanternway Privacy Policy

Applies to the Lanternway apps for iOS and Android. Effective 19 October 2026.

In short: Lanternway has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your location, your searches and your routes stay on your phone. The developer never receives them.

Who we are

Lanternway is an independent app made by Prem Kumar. It gives walking directions in Chicago that suggest routes with fewer reported street crimes at that time of day. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Lanternway.

What the app downloads

Like any app that loads a map, Lanternway downloads files from the internet. Each download tells the server your IP address and the normal technical details of a web request. No account, name or device ID is sent.

  • The weekly risk forecast and street map data, from GitHub Pages (prem-kumar-dev.github.io) or its mirror on jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These are the same public files for every user. The request does not include your location.
  • Map tiles, from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org). Tile requests show which part of the map is on your screen, as with any online map.

We do not run these servers and do not receive their logs. Their operators' privacy policies apply: GitHub, jsDelivr and OpenFreeMap.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or sell personal data.
  • We don't use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't share data with data brokers.
  • We don't use background location. The app cannot see your location when it is closed.
  • We don't require an account, so there is no account to delete.

Calling 911

The "Call 911" button opens your phone's dialer with 911 filled in. The call is made by your phone, not by Lanternway, and the app does not record it.

Where the risk scores come from

Risk scores are computed from public, anonymised City of Chicago crime records (Chicago Data Portal), not from anything Lanternway users do. Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Children

Lanternway is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Please note

Lanternway's routes are lower-risk suggestions based on past reports, not a guarantee of safety. Stay aware of your surroundings, and call 911 in an emergency.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released. The full history of this page is public in the Lanternway repository.
